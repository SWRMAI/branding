01 — The Idea

Vision, repaired in gold.

OpticMind builds distributed computer-vision and remote command systems that see what others miss. Our flagship platform, SWRM, connects thousands of edge sensors into a single, living field of perception.

Kintsugi — the Japanese art of mending with gold — is our governing metaphor. The world is full of gaps in awareness. We trace those fractures and fill them with intelligence, making the whole stronger and more beautiful than before.

10 — Prompts & Agent Skills

Use the brand consistently

Point agents to brand-styles.json, brand-styles.schema.json, and skills/opticmind-brand/SKILL.md before asking for UI, copy, decks, assets, or implementation work.

UI Implementation

Use the OpticMind Labs brand kit. Load brand-styles.json and follow skills/opticmind-brand/SKILL.md. Build a dark, precise, operational interface: Obsidian canvas, Graphite panels, sparse Kintsugi Gold seams and accents, Space Grotesk headings, Manrope body, Cormorant Garamond italic only for brand-moment statements. Keep radii at 6px, reserve gold for signal, dividers, and primary actions, and use kintsugi textures only as low-opacity hero backdrops vignetted into Obsidian.

Copywriting

Write in the OpticMind Labs voice: precise, composed, human. Use specific operational language and measurable outcomes. Lean on the kintsugi idea — fractures traced and filled with intelligence — for brand moments. The slogan is "Turning movements into meaning" (sentence case, no exclamation mark). Avoid hype, vague futurism, fear-based surveillance language, and exaggerated claims. The tone should feel calm, intelligent, and useful.

Visual Asset

Create an OpticMind Labs visual asset: obsidian background, restrained gold seams, engineered composition, high contrast. Use the eye/iris/hexagon motif or the swarm particle ring when it clarifies the message, and the production dome-camera lockup for official applications. Kintsugi photo textures only at low opacity behind heroes. No off-brand blues, playful gradients, pastels, heavy decoration, or blurry stock imagery.
