01 - Identity

Primary mark and lockups

The eye shape carries perception. The iris and hexagon imply structured intelligence. The gold line should feel deliberate and sparse, never ornamental clutter.

03 - Typography

Type system

04 - Motif

Gold connection lines

Use thin, intentional paths to connect sections, map data flows, or imply repair and synthesis. Lines should have purpose and remain secondary to content.

05 - Voice

How the brand speaks

The voice is specific, calm, and human. It avoids vague futurism, exaggerated claims, and surveillance-coded language.

Precise

Use measurable details: latency, coverage, confidence, throughput, impact.

Composed

Prefer calm authority over hype. Show the system doing useful work.

Human

Technology serves people and places. Prefer "connect" and "interpret" over fear-based phrasing.

07 - Prompts and Agent Skills

Use the brand consistently

Point agents to brand-styles.json, brand-styles.schema.json, and skills/opticmind-brand/SKILL.md before asking for UI, copy, decks, assets, or implementation work.

UI Implementation

Use the OpticMind Labs brand kit. Load brand-styles.json and follow skills/opticmind-brand/SKILL.md. Build a dark, precise, operational interface using Obsidian, Graphite panels, sparse Kintsugi Gold accents, Space Grotesk headings, and Manrope body text. Keep radii at 6px, avoid off-palette colors, and reserve gold for signal, dividers, and primary actions.

Copywriting

Write in the OpticMind Labs voice: precise, composed, human. Use specific operational language and measurable outcomes. Avoid hype, vague futurism, fear-based surveillance language, and exaggerated claims. The tone should feel calm, intelligent, and useful.

Visual Asset

Create an OpticMind Labs visual asset using an obsidian background, restrained gold connection lines, engineered composition, and high contrast. Use the eye/iris/hexagon motif only when it clarifies the message. Do not use bright off-brand blues, playful gradients, soft pastel palettes, heavy decoration, or blurred atmospheric stock imagery.
